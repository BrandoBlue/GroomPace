🐾 GroomPace Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 21, 2026

The short version: GroomPace collects nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no servers. Everything you log lives on your device and never leaves it — unless you personally choose to share or export it.

What we collect

Nothing. GroomPace does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. We have no user accounts, no login, and no back-end server that receives your data.

Where your data lives

Everything you enter — groom times, dog names, breeds, styles, notes, before/after photos, achievements, and settings — is stored locally on your device using your browser's or app's on-device storage. It stays on your phone.

Photos

Before/after photos you add are stored on your device only. They are never uploaded anywhere. A photo leaves your device only in the moment you tap Share or Export and choose where to send it — that action, and where it goes, is entirely your choice.

Network use

The app functions fully offline and makes no network requests of its own. There are no third-party trackers, advertising SDKs, or analytics tools embedded in GroomPace.

Sharing & backups you initiate

  • Share image: creates a before/after image and hands it to your device's own share sheet, so you decide where it goes (e.g. Instagram, Messages).
  • Export / Backup: creates a file of your data that you save or send yourself, to move your data to another device. We never see this file.

Deleting your data

Because your data is only on your device, you are always in full control. Use “Reset All Data” in the app, or simply uninstall it, to remove everything permanently.

Children's privacy

GroomPace is a professional tool for working groomers and is not directed at children. Since we collect no data at all, we collect none from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date. Since the app collects no data, any change would only clarify wording — not expand data collection.
